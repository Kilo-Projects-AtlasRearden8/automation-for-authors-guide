# Glossary

Plain English terms used in this guide. If a word bugs you, it's probably here.

**Admin** — the small tasks that keep your author business running: emails,
newsletters, social posts, uploads, bookkeeping.

**API key** — a secret code that lets one program talk to another. This guide
avoids them entirely; nothing here needs an API key.

**Draft** — a version of an email, post, or page that isn't public yet.

**Dry-run** — running a script so it shows what it *would* do without actually
changing anything.

**KDP** — Kindle Direct Publishing, Amazon's free self-publishing platform.
You upload your manuscript and cover there and set your price.

**Local-first** — everything runs on your own computer. Nothing is uploaded to
the cloud by the scripts in this guide.

**Manuscript** — your book's text file (DOCX, PDF, EPUB, etc.) ready to upload.

**Metadata** — the information about your book: title, subtitle, author,
description, keywords, categories, ISBN.

**ISBN** — a unique ID for your book. KDP can assign one free, or you can
supply your own.

**Blurb** — your book's description, the back-cover-style text readers see.

**ARC** — advance reader copy: a pre-publication version sent to readers who
might review it when the book launches.

**Newsletter** — an email you send to readers on your mailing list, usually
regularly, with updates and news.

**Platform** — where you post: Instagram, Facebook, X/Twitter, TikTok,
Bluesky, Mastodon, Threads, LinkedIn.

**Character count** — how many letters, spaces, and symbols are in a post.
Each platform has its own limit.
