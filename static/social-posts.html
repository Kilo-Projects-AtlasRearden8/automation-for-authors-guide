Social Posts

Posting about your book on Instagram, Facebook, X/Twitter, TikTok, Bluesky, Mastodon, Threads, or LinkedIn takes time — especially when you're rewriting the same announcement for every app. This automation drafts all of them from one hook, one link, and your hashtags.

What it does

scripts/draft_social_posts.py creates draft posts for six platforms at once, each with a rough character count so you can see if a post is too long. It never posts anywhere — it just gives you drafts to copy into each app.

Step by step

  1. Try it in dry-run mode:

    python3 scripts/draft_social_posts.py \
  --book "The Massive AI Manual" \
  --hook "New cover reveal — what do you think?" \
  --link "https://example.com/book" \
  --hashtags "#amreading #indieauthor #newbook"

    Review the drafts on screen. Nothing is saved yet.

  2. Write the drafts to a file:

    python3 scripts/draft_social_posts.py \
  --book "The Massive AI Manual" \
  --hook "New cover reveal — what do you think?" \
  --link "https://example.com/book" \
  --hashtags "#amreading #indieauthor #newbook" \
  --apply

    This creates drafts/social-posts-draft.md. Each platform has its own block with an approximate character count.

  3. Copy and post yourself. Open each app, paste the matching block, and hit post. Vary the wording a little for each platform. Never share personal data (addresses, phone numbers, order IDs) in a public post.

Platforms and rough limits

PlatformApprox. limit
X / Twitter~280 characters
Mastodon / Bluesky / Threads~280 characters
Instagram caption~2200 characters
Facebook post~500 characters
TikTok caption~150 characters
LinkedIn post~1300 characters

Character counts are approximate — check each platform's current limit before publishing.
