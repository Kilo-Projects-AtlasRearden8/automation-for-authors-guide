KDP Uploads

KDP (Kindle Direct Publishing) is Amazon's free self-publishing platform. Uploading a book means filling in metadata, uploading your manuscript and cover, and setting a price. This automation doesn't upload for you — it prepares everything so the actual upload takes minutes instead of hours.

What it does

scripts/kdp_upload_prep.py reads a small metadata JSON file, checks it against KDP's public limits (description under 4000 characters, up to 7 keywords, up to 2 categories, valid ISBN shape, accepted file types), and writes a pre-upload checklist plus a machine-readable manifest. It never contacts Amazon and never uploads anything.

Step by step

  1. Look at the example metadata. scripts/books.example.json shows the shape. Change every value to your own and save as scripts/my-book.json.

    No personal data: use your author name (pen names are fine), not your home address or phone number.

  2. Run the prep check (dry-run):

    python3 scripts/kdp_upload_prep.py --metadata scripts/my-book.json

    You'll see a checklist and any issues: too many keywords, a too-long description, an unrecognized file extension, etc.

  3. Fix issues, then write the prep files:

    python3 scripts/kdp_upload_prep.py --metadata scripts/my-book.json --apply

    This writes kdp-prep/kdp-prep-checklist.md and kdp-prep/kdp-prep-manifest.json. Re-run until the checklist says "No issues found."

  4. Upload at KDP yourself. Log into kdp.amazon.com, copy your details from the checklist, upload your manuscript and cover, set your price, and click Publish. The script never touches your KDP account.

KDP limits to remember

FieldLimit
DescriptionUp to 4,000 characters
KeywordsUp to 7
CategoriesUp to 2
ManuscriptDOCX, PDF, EPUB, HTML, TXT, RTF, DOC
CoverJPEG or TIFF recommended; 2500+ pixels on the longest side
ISBN10 or 13 digits (KDP can assign one free)
