Glossary

Plain English terms used in this guide. If a word bugs you, it's probably here.

TermPlain English meaning
AdminThe small tasks that keep your author business running: emails, newsletters, social posts, uploads, bookkeeping.
API keyA secret code that lets one program talk to another. This guide avoids them entirely; nothing here needs an API key.
DraftA version of an email, post, or page that isn't public yet.
Dry-runRunning a script so it shows what it would do without actually changing anything.
KDPKindle Direct Publishing, Amazon's free self-publishing platform. You upload your manuscript and cover there and set your price.
Local-firstEverything runs on your own computer. Nothing is uploaded to the cloud by the scripts in this guide.
ManuscriptYour book's text file (DOCX, PDF, EPUB, etc.) ready to upload.
MetadataThe information about your book: title, subtitle, author, description, keywords, categories, ISBN.
ISBNA unique ID for your book. KDP can assign one free, or you can supply your own.
BlurbYour book's description, the back-cover-style text readers see.
ARCAdvance reader copy: a pre-publication version sent to readers who might review it when the book launches.
NewsletterAn email you send to readers on your mailing list, usually regularly, with updates and news.
PlatformWhere you post: Instagram, Facebook, X/Twitter, TikTok, Bluesky, Mastodon, Threads, LinkedIn.
Character countHow many letters, spaces, and symbols are in a post. Each platform has its own limit.
